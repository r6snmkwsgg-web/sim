── What the main condition establishes (12 streams) ─────────────────────
1. TOKENS ARE DIFFERENTIATED BY REFERENT CLASS: 12/12, z = 23–41,
   measured exclusively on hear events where the hearer could NOT see any
   rich open node (§2.1 asymmetry enforced per event). What a hearer went
   on to find aligns with the emitter-side's production mapping for that
   token far above the token-permuted null. This is the §4.1-confound-3
   killer: an alarm bell cannot produce kind-conditional alignment.
2. CLASS-DISTINCT COHERENCE: 10/12 streams hold ≥2 classes simultaneously
   coherent on different tokens within one population — the signature
   neutral drift cannot fake (amendment a4). The two failures (s7, s9)
   had 3/6 coherent cells but never two distinct-token classes at once in
   the same population; scored FAIL per prereg and counted against.
3. DIVERGENCE: 12/12 streams end isolation with at least one class
   coherent in both populations on different modal tokens. Two dialects,
   same world, every stream.
4. BORROWING AT CONTACT: 11/12 streams show a divergent class where one
   population's qualified share of the other's token rises ≥0.20 and
   holds. In the canonical stream (s1) the east went 0→100% on the west's
   kind2 token within 500 ticks of the corridor opening. Adopter-vs-non-
   adopter trust toward the other side is mixed (s8: −0.24 vs −0.30,
   predicted direction; s5 reversed): the trust-mediation version of the
   borrowing result is NOT established.
5. TRANSMISSION FIDELITY IS THE WEAK JOINT: stable (last-3 defined
   cohorts ≥0.5 in both pops) in only 3/12. The characteristic failure is
   post-contact lexical churn — after the populations merge, the winning
   token is displaced every ~500–750 ticks (s1: t6→t3→t2→t5) and cohort
   fidelity drops to 0. Pre-contact dialects can be stable for thousands
   of ticks; the merged community's lexicon is not.

── §5.2 communicative success: honestly negative ────────────────────────
Contingency is 0.75–0.96 across ALL main streams: a hearer within earshot
of a call is slightly LESS likely to gather near the call's origin within
50 ticks than the same agent at a matched distance from the same point at
a call-free time. Two structural reasons, both visible in the data:
(1) crowding — a call recruits many hearers to one bloomed node, and most
arrivals lose the race; (2) base-rate saturation — the map is dense
enough that "gathers within 4 cells of a given point in 50 ticks" happens
~31% of the time by foraging alone. The information content of calls
shows up in WHAT hearers find (§5.4, z 23–41), not in WHETHER they find.
Per SPEC-M2 §6 the graded verdicts do not gate on §5.2 contingency, but
this number is reported unspun: these tokens name things; they do not
yet measurably feed anyone.

── Ablation battery ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A (no reinforcement): coherence 0/6 in all 12; no divergence; no
  borrowing; FAIL ×12. Expected signature confirmed — outcome
  reinforcement is load-bearing. Residual z ≥ 3 in 4/12 (z 4–8, vs 23–41
  on main): with lexicons frozen, a bloom of kind k still simultaneously
  drives local emissions and local k-gathers, and this spatio-temporal
  clustering survives a full-run within-side token permutation. Disclosed
  as a known small-sample structure in the null; it produces no
  coherence, so nothing downstream survives.
C (no observation channel): identical macro-signature — coherence 0/6 in
  all 12, FAIL ×12 (same residual small-z clustering, 10/12). The
  acquisition path (hear-while-seeing co-occurrence) is what lets a
  newborn ever reach the confidence floor to act on a tip; without it the
  loop never bootstraps. Spec expectation "lexicon dies with founders"
  understates the result: it never forms at all.
B (full observability, vision = signal radius): DEVIATES from the spec's
  expected signature. §5.7 predicted coordination without differentiation
  (shared-percept confound made real); observed is differentiation in
  10/12 (z 4–15, weaker than main's 23–41) with coherence and divergence
  mostly intact — outcome grounding does not need privacy to build
  kind-structured associations. B also collapses demography (25–82
  survivors vs ~200 on main; s9 west pop = 2): universal far sight
  changes foraging and conflict wholesale, so B is not a clean minimal
  pair. The §4.1 shared-percept confound is instead controlled per event
  in measurement: every §5.4/§5.2 hear event requires the hearer could
  not see any rich node. B's contingency > 1 (8/12) is consistent with
  vision, not communication, driving arrivals. Reported as-is: an
  ablation whose observed signature did not match its preregistered
  expectation, with the per-event filter carrying the confound control.

── Post-hoc measurement amendments (all disclosed in metrics-m2.ts) ─────
a1 §5.5: cohort measured at end+1200 while mean lifespan ≈390 — every
   member dead at measurement; the metric returned NaN by construction in
   every run ever executed. Now end+250. No finite fidelity value was
   ever observed under the old offset.
a2 §5.6: borrowed-share scan had no minimum-denominator guard; a 1–2
   member snapshot could read share 1.0. Snapshots with <10 qualified are
   now no-information.
a3 §5.2: control ticks excluded audible calls only in the trailing
   window while success is scored forward — controls were contaminated by
   calls inside the scored window. Exclusion now covers [t−50, t+50].
a4 §5.1: the beats-drift gate compared a fraction (≤1) against
   driftRate+0.2 ≈ 1.14 — unpassable by any possible data. Replaced by
   the discriminating prediction of the same null (single-pool drift
   cannot hold two classes simultaneously coherent on distinct tokens in
   one population). The single-pool concentration probability (~0.94) is
   still printed as context.
